My Climate Footprint

A turn-based browser game about floods and landslides. Buy a house, prepare it with the actions and money you have, and live through the years of weather the game rolls for you.

What you’ll learn

  • Where you live determines which hazards you face.
  • Preparation reduces damage, but the right preparation depends on the hazard.
  • The neighbourhood’s everyday choices change how often disasters happen.

Set in a fictional region inspired by Lower Hutt, Wellington, Aotearoa New Zealand. Check back soon — the game is being built now.
